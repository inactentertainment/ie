    What you’ll buildDraft a one-page business plan and a first-customer experiment.

    Start with the problem, not the logo

    A business offers something that people find useful enough to choose. Before choosing a name, app, or website style, ask who has the problem and what they do about it now. You need to understand the customer before spending heavily on the solution.

    A business plan explains what you offer, whom it helps, how you reach customers, and how the numbers work. Revenue is money the business brings in. Profit is what remains after the relevant costs. Cash flow is the movement of money into and out of the business. These ideas are related, but they are not the same.

    Your first goal is a small test. Talk to potential customers, describe a clear offer, and learn what is missing. AI can help organize notes and draft questions. It cannot replace real customer responses. Use official sources to check registration, permits, and tax requirements for your situation.
