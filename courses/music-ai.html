    Learn the industry before adding the generator

    The music industry connects creative work with listeners. Songs can be written, performed, recorded, released, promoted, and used in many ways. A songwriter, producer, performer, label, publisher, and distributor can do different jobs. One person or team may do more than one of them.

    A song and a recording are different things. Think of a song as the words and musical composition. A recording captures a particular version. That distinction helps explain credits, permissions, and how people are paid. We will introduce the terms slowly before moving into the details.

    Technology has changed the industry many times: printed music, recordings, radio, downloads, streaming, and now generative tools. AI adds new ways to make and shape material. It does not remove the need to listen, choose, explain the project, and check release requirements. Start with the industry map, then explore the deeper AI lessons when the basic roles make sense.
