    Help people understand why your work matters

    Digital marketing uses online channels to help people find and understand an offer. Social media is one part of it. Search, email, websites, and customer follow-up also matter. Posting more often is not a complete strategy. You need a clear audience and a useful message.

    An audience is the group you want to help. Positioning explains why your offer fits their need. A customer journey is the path from first noticing the offer to taking a useful next step. Start with the question a person is trying to answer at each point.

    AI can help draft ideas, outline posts, and organize a content calendar. You review the facts, the voice, and the fit with the audience. Measure actions that matter to the business. A view, a click, an inquiry, and a sale tell you different things.
