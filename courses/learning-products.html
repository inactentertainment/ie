    What you’ll buildDesign one useful learning product and a small feedback test.

    Make something that helps a person learn or act

    A useful digital product helps a person do something they could not do as easily before. It might be a lesson, checklist, journal, template, or small app. Start with the user’s problem and what they already know. The product’s job should be clear before you generate a large amount of content.

    A learning outcome describes what the learner should be able to do. An example shows the skill. Practice lets them try. Feedback helps them improve. A quiz can support learning, but it should not be the only chance to apply the idea. Questions can appear throughout the experience.

    AI can help draft explanations and activities. You check accuracy, reading level, accessibility, and the match with the learner’s need. Then test the product with a real person. A polished file is a beginning. A useful learning experience comes from what the person can understand and apply.

    Understand

    Start with plain words, a useful question, and one worked example.

    Watch

    Hear a perspective, notice a choice, and pause to think.

    Practice

    Try a small task. Use feedback to make the next attempt better.

    Build

    Make something you can use, test it, and reflect on the result.
