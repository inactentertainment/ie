    What you’ll buildDesign and test one small business workflow.

    Give the work a clear path

    A workflow is a sequence of steps that takes work from a request to a result. Think about a customer inquiry. Someone receives it, checks what is needed, prepares information, reviews it, and replies. If the steps are unclear, software can make the confusion move faster.

    Start by drawing the current process. Name the input, the expected result, and the person responsible at each step. Then identify repeated work. AI might draft a summary, sort requests, or prepare a checklist. A fixed rule might be enough for a simpler task.

    An agent is a system that can take steps and use tools within its allowed limits. Its ability to do something is not the same as permission to do it. Keep review points visible. Try incomplete and unusual cases before trusting a process with important actions.
