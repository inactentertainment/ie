# ie
An interactive digital business card for InAct Entertainment LLC—connecting our websites, learning apps, practical tools, AI music, and creative projects in one place.

## Platinum site build — October 11, 2026

Home retains the rotating connected-world wheel, four capability cards, and a department-based branching inquiry. Courses, articles, and Business Word Smith have standalone catalog pages. Every main-site page uses the lower-case word menu, existing music player with rotating record control, monochrome gradient cards, and navigation/legal links above the footer identity and copyright. The entertainment demo remains its separate app.

Home is permanently lights out. All main-site pages start with lights out. Other pages offer an optional lights control at bottom left; read-aloud stays smaller at bottom right. The music box uses jet-black backgrounds.

Business Word Smith uses the existing 429 original terms. Flashcards offer pronunciation playback, speech recognition, favorites, category decks, review scheduling, progress import/export, and voice commands. Mastery requires both a meaning and scenario challenge; an incorrect answer returns that term to practice. Silver awards unlock at 25, 50, 100, 150, and 250 mastered terms, with reduced-motion support and printable awards. Voice recognition is browser-dependent and compares recognized wording, not accent quality. Progress is browser-local.

Contact submissions retain the existing FormSubmit delivery to inactentertainment@gmail.com, include the recommended department, and return to the contact/free-learning section. Initial FormSubmit activation and inbox delivery remain dependent on that service; no fabricated submission receipt is shown.
