Playback & privacy

Your saved favorites and added tracks stay in this browser. No analytics are used by this demo. Audio files are stored locally when browser storage allows. Clearing browser data removes them.

By loading a YouTube player you agree to the YouTube Terms and acknowledge the Google Privacy Policy.

YouTube stops when the console closes or its video is hidden. Compact view keeps the video visible. Direct audio can continue when this console closes; background playback depends on your browser.
