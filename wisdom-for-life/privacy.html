Privacy Policy

Updated November 20, 2026 · This notice applies to the Wisdom for Life prototype.

Wisdom for Life is operated by InAct Entertainment. Questions entered in the present prototype are matched to locally stored educational material using JavaScript; we do not receive or store the question text on a Wisdom for Life server. Theme and reading-voice choices are saved in browser local storage, so your preference may be remembered on the same device. Speech recognition and text-to-speech are supplied by your device or browser; depending on its implementation, a browser or operating-system provider may process audio or text. We have not activated Google AdSense, third-party analytics, behavioral advertising or advertising cookies on these Wisdom for Life pages. Hosting and network services may process technical logs. Linked third-party pages follow their own privacy notices. Before enabling ads or other data-processing tools, we must update this policy to identify providers and purposes and implement legally required consent. Avoid entering passwords, government identification numbers, and confidential medical or financial records. Contact the operator using the InAct Entertainment website.
