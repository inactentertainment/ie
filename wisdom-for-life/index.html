History & heritage

The roots of faith

Explore different historical beginnings and important connections. Older does not mean better. Many traditions developed gradually, and their exact starting dates cannot be established.

ॐ

Hinduism

Ancient South Asia · no single founding date

Vedic texts are attested in the second millennium BCE; Hindu traditions developed over many centuries.

✡

Judaism

Ancient Israel and Judah · gradual development

Rooted in ancient Israelite religion, with distinctive Jewish traditions developing over time.

☸

Buddhism

South Asia · approximately fifth century BCE

Associated with the teachings of Siddhartha Gautama, the Buddha.

✺

Yoruba / Ifá

West Africa · original date unknown

An indigenous African religious tradition, preserving extensive oral teachings and divination literature. No secure foundation date is known.

✝

Christianity

First century CE · Roman Judea

Emerging from a Jewish context and centered on the life and teachings of Jesus.

☪

Islam

Seventh century CE · Arabia

Rooted in the Qur'anic revelations received by the Prophet Muhammad.

These cards provide broad historical context, not a definitive oldest-to-youngest ranking. Yoruba/Ifá and Hinduism do not have reliably established founding years. Judaism, Christianity and Islam share important Abrahamic connections; Hinduism and Buddhism arose in South Asia. Similar moral themes do not mean identical beliefs about God.
Our promise

Personal, respectful, and source-led

Wisdom for Life aims to help people find meaningful teachings for everyday problems without favoring one religion or pretending all beliefs are the same. References link to source sites; the explanations here are paraphrases, not verbatim translations.

For a fully open-ended experience, a secure backend, licensed or public-domain source library, retrieval and review process, and optional AI/voice services must be added. The site never claims to be God, a prophet, or a religious authority.

Educational reflection only, not professional medical, mental-health, legal or financial advice. For immediate danger, contact local emergency services.
